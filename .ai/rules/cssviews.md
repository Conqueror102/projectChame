# Cssviews

## Use the Project Cham pink and blue palette
The approved brand anchors are awareness pink #EB3761 and medical blue #0A63C7. Use the cham-pink-* and cham-blue-* scales plus cham-primary/cham-secondary semantic tokens from resources/css/app.css; use charcoal, blush, sky, white, and cool gray neutrals derived there. Do not reintroduce the old green/yellow palette or raw brand hex values in components.

## Apply the approved palette by role
Use deep navy (#0A2344) for dark blue sections and event surfaces; charcoal (#242429) for the header, structural bars, and primary text; pink (#EB3761) for emphasis and primary actions; bright blue (#0A63C7) for secondary actions and interactive accents. Use light blue and light pink for soft surfaces, with white and black only where contrast requires them. Do not introduce additional brand colors.
