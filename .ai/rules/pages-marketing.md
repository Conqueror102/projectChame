# Pages Marketing

## Dedicated About page uses seven distinct editorial sections
The `/about` page is a full dedicated marketing page, not the homepage About fragment. Preserve its seven-part structure: documentary split hero, layered story collage, dark philosophy framework, mission/vision diptych, staggered values cards, outcome-led difference ledger, and pink closing promise. Do not use a generic “About Us” hero title; keep Project Cham content, approved palette, spacious gutters, and Varela/Indie Flower/Noto typography roles.
